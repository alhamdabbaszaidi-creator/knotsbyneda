FAQ

Do you take custom orders?

Yes! You can request custom colors, sizes, and styles.

How long does an order take?

Most pieces take 3–7 days depending on size and complexity.

Do you ship?

Yes, worldwide shipping is available.

How does payment work?

Orders are placed after you sign in, so you can always see their status in "My Orders." PayPal is confirmed instantly. Zelle and CashApp are confirmed by hand once Neda sees the payment land — usually within a day.
